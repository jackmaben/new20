14 FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does custom software development cost?

Custom software development typically costs $20,000 to $250,000 or more. Simple applications may cost $20,000–$50,000, while complex platforms can exceed $250,000. Development costs depend on features, technical complexity, integrations, team location, security requirements, and project duration. Ongoing maintenance commonly adds 15–20% of the initial development cost per year.

How long does custom software development take?

Custom software development typically takes 3–12 months. Simple applications can take 2–4 months, while complex platforms may require 6–18 months or longer. Development time depends on project scope, features, integrations, team size, testing requirements, security needs, and the speed of stakeholder feedback and approvals.

Which engagement model is best for my software project?

The best software development engagement model depends on project scope, budget certainty, and requirement flexibility. Use fixed price for clearly defined projects, time and materials for evolving requirements, and a dedicated team for long-term or complex development. Choose the model that matches your project’s level of change and control.

Do I need complete requirements before starting development?

No, you do not need complete requirements before starting software development. Define the core business goals, essential features, target users, and initial scope before development begins. Agile development allows Optimize Tech Studio to refine detailed requirements through feedback and testing as the project progresses.

How many developers will work on my project?

Most software projects use a team of 2–8 developers, depending on scope, complexity, timeline, and technical requirements. A small MVP may need 2–3 developers, while a complex platform may require 5–10+ specialists. Optimize Tech Studio can scale the development team as project needs change.

Can you work with our existing internal development team?

Yes, we can work with your existing internal development team. External developers can extend team capacity, provide specialized expertise, accelerate development, and support specific project phases. A shared workflow, defined responsibilities, regular communication, and compatible development standards help internal and external developers collaborate effectively.

How do you communicate with US clients during development?

We communicate with US clients through scheduled video meetings, email, Slack or Microsoft Teams, and project management tools. The development team establishes overlapping working hours for real-time discussions and provides regular progress updates. Clear documentation, defined communication channels, and scheduled reviews keep stakeholders aligned throughout development.

Can existing software be modernized instead of replaced?

Yes, existing software can often be modernized instead of completely replaced. Optimize Tech Studio can refactor legacy code, upgrade frameworks, modernize interfaces, migrate infrastructure, improve security, and integrate new features. Modernization can preserve valuable business logic while reducing technical debt, improving performance, and extending the software’s useful life.
